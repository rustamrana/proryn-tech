Who We Are
About PRORYN TECH

PRORYN TECH Private Limited is an enterprise software development company headquartered in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, India. Founded in 2024, we specialize in building secure, scalable, and intelligent software solutions for startups, SMEs, enterprises, and government organizations across 10+ industries.

We deliver end-to-end technology services — from enterprise software development and AI automation to IT infrastructure, system integration, cloud services, cybersecurity, and managed support — backed by a team with 10+ years of enterprise engineering experience.

Our flagship product, PRORYN BusinessOS, is an AI-powered Business Operating System designed to unify enterprise operations on a single intelligent platform.
