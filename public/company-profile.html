About PRORYN TECH
We are a technology company founded in 2024 in Bhopal, India, with over 10 years of combined leadership experience. We build intelligent software, deploy infrastructure, and deliver end-to-end IT solutions for businesses across government, enterprise, and startup sectors.

To empower organizations with intelligent, secure, and scalable technology solutions that drive measurable business outcomes. We deliver software that works — on time, within budget, and built to evolve alongside your business.

OUR JOURNEY
Company Journey
Over a decade of building, delivering, and innovating — from foundational engineering work to launching an intelligent software platform for modern businesses.
2015
Engineering Foundation
The leadership team began its technology journey building enterprise software solutions and gaining deep expertise in custom application development, system architecture, and database engineering for private sector clients.
2017
Government Projects & Public Sector Entry
Expanded into government and public sector technology projects, delivering e-governance solutions, digital citizen services portals, and secure IT infrastructure for state-level departments.
2019
Enterprise Leadership
Took on leadership roles in enterprise-scale deployments — ERP implementations, large-scale system integration projects, IT infrastructure rollouts, and managed services contracts spanning multiple industries.
2022
AI & Automation Expertise
Deep investment in AI, automation, and intelligent workflow technologies. Delivered AI-powered solutions, RPA implementations, and machine learning integrations for enterprise clients seeking to automate complex business processes.
2024
PRORYN TECH PRIVATE LIMITED Founded
Formally incorporated PRORYN TECH PRIVATE LIMITED in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh — bringing together a decade of expertise into a single, focused technology company committed to delivering intelligent software and IT solutions to businesses across India.
2025
PRORYN BusinessOS Launch
Launched PRORYN BusinessOS — a comprehensive all-in-one business management platform with modules for CRM, Sales, Inventory, HRMS, Projects, Helpdesk, Document Management, Workflow Automation, Analytics, and an integrated AI Assistant.
